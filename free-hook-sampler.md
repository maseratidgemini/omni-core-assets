### 1. The Pattern Interrupt

### 2. The Open Loop
* **The Hook:** "I tested [X] for 30 days, and the results at the end completely shocked me."
* **Why it works:** Teases a high-stakes outcome at the end of the video, creating an urgent desire to watch through to the payoff.

### 3. The Authority Gap
* **The Hook:** "99% of creators fail at this because they ignore one simple rule."

### 4. The Speed Paradox
* **The Hook:** "Stop spending hours editing. Do this in 15 minutes instead."
* **Why it works:** Directly addresses a massive pain point (wasted time) and offers an immediate shortcut.

* **Why it works:** Sparks curiosity and engagement through a bold, unexpected perspective.
